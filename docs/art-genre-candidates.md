## What is and is not established

- The positive classifications above are source-supported. This does not prove
  their negative comparisons against every other work.
- Two added teaching reserves are a proposal, not an assertion that replacing
  two facts suffices. Additional answer refinements and evidence may still be
  needed, especially for B/C review selections.
- This is the smallest **concrete starting proposal found in this pass**, not
  a proven globally minimal replacement set or a release-readiness claim.
- No alternative should become a distractor until its exact work-specific
  negative is verified. Siblings in a genre hierarchy are not automatically
  disjoint. Broader labels stay valid.
- Any approved candidate must subsequently pass joint six-fact composition,
  every four-pair permanent-lock completion, both delivery modes and complete
  fresh progression. Individual positive facts and option counts do not certify
  those outcomes.

The immediate decision is whether to author these narrow answer refinements /
reserves while retaining the original records, or keep the existing canonical
answers and continue to report their evidence shortages. Neither path authorises
discarding content, changing priorities silently, committing or pushing.
